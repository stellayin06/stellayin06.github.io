I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at Nanyang Technological University and A*STAR, Singapore. I study how people learn, seek information, and think with AI, and design systems that support them.
I received my Ph.D. in Information Studies from Nanyang Technological University, where I was advised by Professors Dion Hoe-Lian Goh and Choon Lang Quek. Prior to that, I earned a Bachelor's degree from Sun Yat-sen University, China, and a Master's degree from the University of Manchester, UK.

01 / RESEARCH

Research Focus.

Connecting information science, learning sciences, empirical research, and AI system design to understand and support human learning and thinking.

✳01

Human–AI Collaboration

Investigating human–AI collaboration practices and the factors that shape them, developing strategies for productive and constructive collaboration, and designing systems that support critical thinking and reflective reasoning.

Explore the work ↗
⌁02

Learning Analytics

Developing multimodal and AI-driven learning analytics that integrate dialogue, behavior, emotion, attention, and log data to understand how collaboration unfolds and identify opportunities to support learning.

Explore the work ↗
◈03

AI for Education

Designing pedagogically grounded conversational tutors, learner simulations, and adaptive scaffolding to support personalized learning across diverse learners and educational contexts.
